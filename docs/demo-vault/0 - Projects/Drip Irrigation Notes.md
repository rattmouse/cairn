# Drip Irrigation Notes

Half-inch mainline down the centre path, quarter-inch spurs to each bed. Emitters are 2 L/h, which at the pump timings in [[Greenhouse Automation]] works out to roughly a cup per plant per cycle.

## Layout

- Mainline: 14 m, one pressure regulator at the tap
- Spurs: 6, punched not cut
- Emitters: 34 total

## Things learned the hard way

1. Punch the holes *before* pressurising the line.
2. A filter costs less than a season of clogged emitters.
3. Flush the line each spring — silt collects at the far end.
